Product pages wait for Recommendations, and calls to it take up to 30 s. Yet the data team's inference histogram says p99 < 40 ms. Both are true: a histogram of finished work says nothing about the work that waited, or never finished.

Look at Recommendations from the inside, in Prometheus: how many workers it has (`recs_workers`), how many are busy (`recs_workers_busy`), how many requests wait for one (`recs_queue_length`), and how long answered requests took from arrival to response (`recs_request_duration_seconds`). Then read its logs: which requests take a worker for seconds, and what happens to them?
