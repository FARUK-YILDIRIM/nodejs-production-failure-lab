Requests that never finish never show up in a latency histogram: the API only measures the product pages it managed to answer. Compare the edge (*Requests per second by status*: what are the 499s?) with the API's own view.

Then find out what the abandoned requests are doing. Look at *Calls to other services*, and at the API's *Active libuv resources*: how many sockets are open, and to whom? A product page trace in Jaeger shows where its time goes, though you may have to wait for one to finish.
