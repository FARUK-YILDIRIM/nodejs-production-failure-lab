`dns.lookup()` (which every new connection uses) runs `getaddrinfo` on libuv's thread pool, and libuv lets "slow I/O" such as DNS use at most half of that pool: 2 of the 4 default threads. Two lookups at a time, ~0.5 s each, three orders per second: the queue never drains.

TaxRate's DNS being slow isn't something you can fix. The question is why every single quote needs a lookup at all. Read how the TaxRate client connects, and why it was written that way.
