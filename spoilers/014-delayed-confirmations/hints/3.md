The worker handles one message at a time (`eachMessage`), and each one waits ~650 ms for MailWave since MailWave moved: at most ~1.5 emails per second. Starting more workers wouldn't help: the topic has one partition, and a partition is read by a single consumer of the group at a time.

Emails don't depend on each other. Process many messages of a batch at once (`eachBatch`), and mark the batch as done (`resolveOffset`) only when all of them are handled. Or give the topic more partitions and consume them concurrently. Either way, think about what happens to messages in flight if the worker stops halfway.
