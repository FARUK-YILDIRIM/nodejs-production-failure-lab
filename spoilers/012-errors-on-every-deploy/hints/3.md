Shut down in two steps. First, make `/ready` answer 503, and keep serving as usual for longer than the proxy's check interval (say 5 s), so that the proxy stops sending requests here before anything is refused. Answering with `Connection: close` meanwhile keeps the proxy from reusing connections that are about to close.

Then close the server, let the requests in flight finish, close the database pool and exit, all within Docker's 10 s grace period.
