An instance that receives SIGTERM calls `server.close()` right away: it refuses new connections, and closes its idle keep-alive connections. It exits a few milliseconds later. But the proxy only learns that an instance is going away from its readiness check, every 2 seconds, and its `/ready` passed a moment ago. Until the next check fails, the proxy keeps sending that instance half of the requests: connection refused, or a connection closed under its feet. 502.

What should the instance tell the proxy, and keep doing, during those seconds?
