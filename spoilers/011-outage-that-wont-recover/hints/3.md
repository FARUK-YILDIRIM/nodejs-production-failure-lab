Before 14:02, 3 checkouts per second meant 3 quotes per second. During the failover every quote failed, and each checkout became up to 16 requests (4 attempts by the app × 4 by the API): about 48 per second, twice what TaxRate can do. The queue filled with work nobody waits for, so quotes kept failing after the failover was over, and kept being retried. The failure feeds itself, and a restart of the API doesn't empty TaxRate's queue.

Retries must not multiply the load when things fail. Retry at most once, after a random pause, and only while retries stay a small share of all the quotes you ask for: a retry budget. Backoff alone isn't enough: it spreads the same retries over time without removing any.
