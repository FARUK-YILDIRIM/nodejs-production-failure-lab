The API is frozen but idle, so it's waiting on something. Look at the **Database connection pool** panel during a freeze, right after a `./lab deploy`: how many connections are open, how many are idle, and how many requests are waiting for one?

Then look at the database side of those connections. In `./lab psql`:

```sql
SELECT pid, state, wait_event_type, wait_event, now() - xact_start AS in_transaction_for, query
  FROM pg_stat_activity
 WHERE datname = 'shop' AND backend_type = 'client backend';
```
