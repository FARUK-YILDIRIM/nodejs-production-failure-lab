Look at what is retained. Right after a restart (the heap is small, so snapshots are quick), open `chrome://inspect`, connect to `127.0.0.1:9229`, and take a heap snapshot in the **Memory** tab. Take a second one a minute later and switch to the **Comparison** view: which objects are added by the tens of thousands? Open a few and follow their retainers up to a `Map`. What are its keys?

Then compare those keys with the URLs the campaign traffic sends. How many distinct keys can it produce, and when does an entry ever leave the map?
