The heap climbs until it hits the V8 heap limit (~259 MB here), and the process aborts. More traffic or bigger pages alone would produce a busier sawtooth, not a floor that keeps rising: look at **live heap (after full GC)**. Something keeps references to objects that should have become garbage.
