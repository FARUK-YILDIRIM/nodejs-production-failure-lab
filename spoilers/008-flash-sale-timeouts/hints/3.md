Since 2.23.0, the checkout transaction locks the products in the cart, then asks TaxRate for the tax and PayFlow for a card authorization (half a second together) before it writes the order and commits. All week, orders were spread over 20,000 products and their locks never met. Today almost every order contains one of three doorbusters, so orders for the same doorbuster go through one at a time, about two per second, while the others wait in line. Each of them holds one of the API's ten database connections, so product pages and order history wait for a connection too.

Keep slow calls out of transactions. Ask PayFlow first, then open a short transaction that takes the stock with a single statement that only succeeds if enough is left (`UPDATE … WHERE stock >= $2`) and writes the order. If the order can't be written after all, don't leave the authorization on the customer's card: PayFlow can void it.
